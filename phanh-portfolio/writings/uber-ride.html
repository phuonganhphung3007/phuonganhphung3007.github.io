Predicting Uber fares from trip features

Results summary for uber_trip_main.ipynb (version of 3 Oct 2026). A linear regression (OLS) of fare_amount on trip distance, time of day, a US-holiday flag and city, fitted on 50,000 trips (40,000 to train, 10,000 held out to test).

Summary

  • The model explains about 76% of the variation in fares (R² 0.759 on training data) and 75.3% on trips it never saw, so it generalises well.
  • Predictions are off by about $2.49 per trip on average (mean absolute error on the test set).
  • Distance does nearly all the work. Each kilometre adds about $1.85 (95% CI $1.84–$1.86), on top of a base of about $3.06.
  • Time of day has no measurable effect (p = 0.45), and holidays add only about $0.10, which is not significant at the 5% level (p = 0.07).
  • City barely matters. Compared with Boston, only Los Angeles differs significantly, by about −$0.16 per trip.
  • Exploration findings: trips are spread evenly across the six cities, and every city has the same status mix: roughly 85% completed, 10% cancelled and 5% no-show. The distance-vs-fare scatter fans out as distance grows, so fares on longer trips vary more.

# --- Holiday flag (cell 13) ---
unique_years = df_uber['pickup_time'].dt.year.unique()
country_holidays = holidays.US(years=unique_years)
df_uber['is_holiday'] = df_uber['pickup_time'].dt.date.apply(lambda d: d in country_holidays)
df_uber['is_holiday'] = df_uber['is_holiday'].astype(int)

# --- Feature: time of day in seconds (cell 15) ---
df_uber['second'] = df_uber['pickup_only_time'].apply(lambda t: (t.hour * 3600) + (t.minute * 60) + t.second)
fare_pred_num_features = ['second', 'distance_km', 'is_holiday']

# --- Encode categories, split 80/20, add intercept (cell 16) ---
df_uber_encoded = pd.get_dummies(df_uber, columns = ['city', 'pickup_only_time'], drop_first=True, dtype=int)
fare_predictors = fare_pred_num_features + [col for col in df_uber_encoded.columns if 'city' in col]
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(df_uber_encoded[fare_predictors], df_uber_encoded['fare_amount'], test_size=0.2, random_state=0)
X_train = sm.add_constant(X_train)

# --- Fit OLS and inspect (cells 17–18) ---
# Fit the OLS model
model = sm.OLS(y_train, X_train).fit()

print(model.summary())
coefficients = model.params
print(f'Feature coefficients: {coefficients}')

# --- R² on training data (cell 20) ---
# R-squared value
r_squared = model.rsquared
print(f'R-squared: {r_squared}')

# --- Test-set R² with scikit-learn (cells 21–22) ---
regressor = LinearRegression()
regressor.fit(X_train,y_train)

X_test = sm.add_constant(X_test)
r2_score = regressor.score(X_test,y_test)
print(r2_score*100,'%')

# --- Final evaluation on held-out trips (cell 23) ---
y_pred = model.predict(X_test)

from sklearn.metrics import r2_score, mean_absolute_error
print('Test R²:', r2_score(y_test, y_pred))
print('Test MAE ($):', mean_absolute_error(y_test, y_pred))
